Production recommendation

The sheet you send to the coil maker

Duty, geometry, fluid, hydraulics, materials and the icing service on one page. The geometry is a proposal: a manufacturer re-rates it in its own selection program and may offer its own fin and tube pattern. Ask for that rating at both duty points.

02

Cooling load · chiller power

How big the cold side must be

In a closed loop every watt the fan puts into the air comes back as heat, together with the walls and the spray water that freezes. The coil has to remove all of it at the iced point, when ice on the vanes and fins has pushed the circuit loss up to the fan's limit λfan. This part updates as you type; it does not wait for the coil search.

Fluids and alternatives

Every fluid, its best coil

For each fluid the search keeps the coil that lets the chiller run warmest, then checks the fluid's own limits: lowest use temperature, freezing margin at the evaporator wall, and the CO₂ cascade limit. 04

Ice through the loop

Where the spray water goes

Droplets the model does not catch fly on through the corner vanes, the fan and finally the coil. Heated corner vanes keep their ice-free shape, so more water reaches the coil. The front-stage fin pitch is sized so that the leading-edge ice horns do not close the gap before the run ends in the worst sizing case.
